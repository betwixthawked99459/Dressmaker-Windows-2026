## 🕹️ About

Dressmaker is a cozy crafting and simulation game developed by Cozy Lives and published by Free Lives. Run a dressmaking shop, choose fabrics, design patterns, cut and sew garments, then fulfill commissions for townsfolk with different tastes and occasions. This repository contains an unofficial leaked pre-release Windows build and is not affiliated with the developers or publisher.

A relaxing creative simulation for players who enjoy cozy games, crafting, fashion design, and hands-on customization.

> ⚠️ **This is an unofficial leaked pre-release build.** The game is under active development: expect bugs, unfinished content, and balance changes.Archive is password protected (`2026`) to compatibility automated DMCA bots and hosting service scanners that would otherwise delete the files within hours.

## ✨ Features

- 🧵 **Choose Fabrics** — Work with materials including cotton, linen, wool, silk, and velvet.
- 📐 **Create Patterns** — Design garments in your sketchbook and arrange pattern pieces.
- ✂️ **Cut Precisely** — Cut fabric according to your chosen layout and design.
- 🪡 **Sew Garments** — Guide fabric through the sewing machine to assemble each dress.
- ✨ **Add Decorations** — Customize dresses with buttons, bows, appliqué, lace, and accessories.
- 🏘️ **Serve Townsfolk** — Create custom dresses for customers and their important events.
- 💰 **Grow Your Reputation** — Complete commissions to earn reputation and coin, or sell designs from your shop window.
- 🐈 **Pet the Kitty** — Take a cozy break to pet the shop cat.
